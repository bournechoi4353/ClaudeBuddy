Pet brain

The raincloud reacts to what you are doing and says a short line now and then. Lines come from a built-in set. Nothing leaves this machine.
Scales how long the pet waits between bubbles.
A small language model on this Mac writes the line instead of picking one from the built-in set. It runs here and nothing leaves this machine. It uses about 1.5 GB of memory while loaded and unloads after 10 minutes without a line. The first line after it loads or unloads still comes from the built-in set.
Off by default. When on, the pet sends only a rounded latitude and longitude (about 1 km) to Open-Meteo.com every 15 minutes or more, with no account or identifier, so the cloud can match the weather when it is idle. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to them. Typing a city sends that name to Open-Meteo once, when you press Find.
